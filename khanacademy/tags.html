No one is born knowing everything. We all begin from zero. We cant speak, walk, or solve equations.
Reading, writing, counting, learning to ride a bike — no one is great at something right away.
Even Einstein once struggled with simple numbers.
Shakespeare also had to learn the alphabet like everyone else.
Luckily, learning is part of being human.
Step by step. Slowly but surely. You try, fall, get up, and try again.
It can be hard. Confusing. Exhausting.
But one day, you succeed.
One step after another. One idea building on the next.
Every mistake helps your brain grow stronger.
Failure is not the end — it is part of progress. So you continue.
This is learning.
It doesnt mean youre incapable.
It simply means you havent understood it yet.
Because the most amazing and complex ideas in the world
are created from simple concepts anyone can learn.
No matter who you are or where you come from.

Remember just one thing:
You are capable of learning anything.
